A few helpful details

Good to know.

Have another question? Send Tina a note and she’ll be happy to help.

How many guests can be painted in an hour?

As a guide, around 10 designs per hour. Timing depends on the size and detail of the designs chosen, and how many guests are ready when their turn comes.

What products do you use?

I use professional face-paint brands such as Fusion Body Art, TAG and Superstar. Products are selected for cosmetic use and applied according to the manufacturer's directions.

How should face paint be removed?

Start with lukewarm water and a soft washcloth, following the directions for the product used. Avoid harsh scrubbing. For colours that need extra help, use a remover recommended by the paint manufacturer.

What if my child has sensitive skin?

Please let me know before painting if your child has known skin sensitivities or allergies. A child should always feel comfortable choosing a design or deciding not to be painted.

How do I secure a booking?

Send a booking enquiry using the form. I’ll confirm availability and event details with you. A deposit is required to secure the booking; the remaining balance is due before the event begins.
